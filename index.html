Running Club App

Delete Your Account

You can delete your account directly in the app, or request account deletion by contacting support outside the app.

Delete Your Account In The App

  1. Open the Running Club App and sign in.
  2. Open the menu and go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account in the Account section.
  4. Confirm Delete Account when the app asks you to confirm.

Request Deletion Outside The App

If you cannot access the app, email zayaan@talentcore.co.za and ask for your Running Club App account to be deleted. Include the email address or username linked to your account so the request can be verified.

What Happens When Your Account Is Deleted

Your app login account and profile details are removed, and you are signed out. Some club administration, safety, attendance, event, race, or legal records may be retained where required to operate the club or meet recordkeeping obligations.
